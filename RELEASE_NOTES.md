Tibia Toolkit 0.6.0

- Added the Books library, with searchable filters, document details, appearances, and in-app map locations.
- Added the Wheel of Destiny planner, including vocation and promotion controls, import/export codes, selection details, perk summaries, and guided help.
- Expanded Mini World Changes with refresh controls, world-status visuals, Yasir integration, offline guides, and a guided tutorial.
- Reorganized the Library and Tools navigation for faster access to calculators, hunting tools, the Wheel of Destiny, and Tibia Mirror.
- Added the recent Tibia news shortcut and guided tutorials for the new Library, Books, Wheel of Destiny, and Mini World Changes screens.
- Improved Tibia Mirror interaction and alert reliability, including mirror selection, context menus, deletion responsiveness, and visual and sound feedback.
- Updated translations and content handling across Portuguese, English, and German while preserving Tibia proper names.
